Frequently asked questions

Where does my reversed video go?

Nowhere until you choose: on the result, tap “Save” to add it to your Photos library, or “Share” to send it. The first time you save, iOS asks for permission to add to your Photos. If you refused, tap “Open Settings” in the app, or go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Reverse Clips, and choose “Add Photos Only”.

Can I reverse without the sound?

Yes. On the preview, turn off “Reversed sound”: the reversed video has no sound. To change the default for every video, open Settings (gear on the home screen) and turn off “Reversed sound by default”.

Does it work with Live Photos?

Yes. Choose a Live Photo in the picker: its video is reversed, like any other video.

My video is long

There is no length limit: long videos are reversed piece by piece, and the screen shows the progress and the time left. Reversing may take several minutes; shorten the selection to go faster. Make sure your device has enough free space. If the video is stored in iCloud, it has to be downloaded first: check your connection.

Reverse Clips is not in the share sheet

The share extension appears when you share a single video. If you don’t see it, scroll to the end of the row of apps, tap “More”, and look for Reverse Clips in the list.

Reversing from Photos or Shortcuts opens the app

Reversing directly in the share sheet or in a shortcut, without opening the app, is a Premium feature. Without Premium, your video opens in Reverse Clips, where you can reverse it for free.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings (gear on the home screen) and tap “Restore purchase”, or tap the same button on the Premium screen (crown on the home screen). Use the Apple Account you bought with. If you bought it in an earlier version of the app, it is still yours.

How do I change my ad choices?

In Settings, tap “Manage ad consent” (shown only where Google’s consent platform requires it). To turn off ad tracking, go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See also “Changing your choices” in the privacy policy.
