Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

In short: Reverse Clips has no account and no server of its own. Your videos are reversed on your device and never leave it, unless you share them yourself. The app only gets the video you pick, and only asks to add videos to your library, never to read it. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects data to serve and measure ads, and personalises them only if you agree. Premium users see no ads.

1. Who is responsible

Reverse Clips and this website are published by TecKnologikS, data controller under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact for any question about your data: support@tecknologiks.com.

2. Your videos

  • Choosing a video: you pick a video or a Live Photo in the system photo picker. The app receives only the item you pick (for a Live Photo, its video), never the rest of your library, and does not ask for permission to read your library. If the video is stored in iCloud, it is downloaded from iCloud by iOS first.
  • Reversing: the video is trimmed and reversed on your device. It is never sent to TecKnologikS or to anyone else: the app has no server.
  • Saving: when you tap “Save”, the app asks for permission to add to your Photos library only (“Add Photos Only”). It cannot read your photos and videos with this permission.
  • Sharing: when you tap “Share”, the video goes through the iOS share sheet to the app or the person you choose, under their own privacy policy.
  • Temporary files: the copies needed for reversing are kept in the app’s own storage on your device. They are deleted when you leave the result, and at the next launch if the app was closed before.

3. Share sheet and Shortcuts

When you reverse a video from the Photos share sheet (“Reverse Clips”) or with the Shortcuts action “Reverse a video”, the video is copied into the temporary storage of the app or of its share extension, on your device. With Premium, it is reversed right there; the reversed copy is deleted when you close the share sheet, and the file returned to Shortcuts is removed once the shortcut is done. Without Premium, the video is handed to the app (from the share sheet, through a storage space shared only by the app and its share extension), and the app opens it like a video picked in the app. Nothing is sent outside your device.

4. Data stored on your device

Your setting “Reversed sound by default” and the Premium status (kept so that the right state shows at launch, even offline) are stored only on your device, in preferences shared by the app and its share extension. They are never sent to TecKnologikS. Deleting the app erases them.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner on the home screen and a full-screen ad while a video is being reversed (the reversing goes on behind the ad). Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the only third-party library in the app. Premium users see no ads: no ad is requested for them. Your consent

  • Where Google’s consent platform requires it (in particular the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown at launch. You can accept, refuse or choose by purpose. If you refuse, ads may still be shown, without personalisation.
  • Then, iOS asks whether the app may use your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). If you refuse, the advertising identifier is not shared.
  • These two steps take place at launch for everyone, Premium included; the Google Mobile Ads SDK is then started as soon as consent allows it. With Premium, no ad is ever requested or shown.

6. Premium purchase

Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Apple (App Store, StoreKit). The payment is processed by Apple under its own privacy policy: the app and TecKnologikS receive no payment data. The app only checks with the App Store whether Premium is active on your Apple Account, at launch and when you buy or restore.

8. Legal bases

  • Your consent: personalised ads, the advertising identifier, and storing or reading information on your device for advertising.
  • Our legitimate interest: showing non-personalised ads that keep the app free, and measuring and protecting them against fraud.
  • The performance of the contract: handling your Premium purchase through Apple.

9. How long

  • Your videos: the app keeps no copy once you leave the result; what you save stays in your Photos library.
  • Settings and Premium status: on your device, until you delete the app.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own retention rules.
  • Purchase data: kept by Apple under its own rules.

10. Changing your choices and deleting your data

  • Consent for ads: on the home screen, tap the Settings button (gear, top right), then “Manage ad consent” in the Privacy section to open the consent form again and change or withdraw your choice at any time. This section appears only where Google’s consent platform requires it.
  • Advertising identifier: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, then turn off Reverse Clips.
  • Photos: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Reverse Clips.
  • Settings and Premium status: deleting the app erases everything it stored on your device. Your purchase stays attached to your Apple Account and can be restored.

TecKnologikS has no account and no user database, so there is nothing else to delete on our side.

11. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to, restrict and port your data, and to withdraw your consent. To exercise them, write to support@tecknologiks.com. For data held by Google or Apple, you can also contact them directly. You can lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr).

12. Children

TecKnologikS does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Parents can turn off tracking in iOS Settings and refuse personalised ads in the consent form.

13. This website

This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party resources.

14. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated.
